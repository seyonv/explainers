Study plan · Day 9

Operate and price a fleet

Day 9 of the inference study plan, the last: many replicas behind one API, what a token really costs, and how to run the whole thing safely. It covers Part 5 of the request-scheduler course, the cost-of-a-token half of the Training and cost per token course and Serving station 7, and ends with the Serving guide's last project and capstone.

By the end of all of this, you'll be able to
  • Route requests across many replicas, and decide when to add or remove one.
  • Explain cold starts and scale to zero, and when routing a request to the replica that already holds its KV pays.
  • Draw the whole serving system from the API gateway to the GPU, and say what each layer is for.
  • Work out the cost of a million tokens at a latency target, and how the batch size moves it.
  • Choose hardware for a workload from its compute, memory bandwidth, memory size and price.
  • Plan a safe release: canaries, failures and quality checks.
The same routine every phase
  1. Prime (5–10 min): skim headings and each card's Prime box, nothing else.
  2. Pretest: try the phase's checkpoint cold. Getting it wrong tells your brain what to look for.
  3. Read in order, and do each card's Retrieve questions with the card closed.
  4. Take the checkpoint for real. Open “Go deeper” only for cards whose questions you missed.
