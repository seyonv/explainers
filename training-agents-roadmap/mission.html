The mission · Training agents with RL

Safecracker: beat frontier models with a 1.7B model you trained

Train a 1.7B model to crack safes it has never seen, and beat frontier models at it, per safe and per dollar. This page is the finished result, written as the post you'll make at the end. Every stage of the roadmap fills in one rung.

Why this is worth posting

You redesigned the game until the shortcut died

With the wiring visible, every safe is a linear system mod 10. A closed-form solver matched the breadth-first-search optimum on 200 of 200 safes, so a frontier model could just do the algebra. Hiding the wiring is what makes it a real agent task.

The test safes are bigger than anything it trained on

Train on 3 and 4 dials; test on 5 and 6. Wiring shapes in the test set never appear in training, checked up to relabelling the dials.

The claim is per dollar, not just a score

Cost per safe cracked puts a 1.7B model you trained and a frontier API on the same axis, with your GPU hours on one side and their token bill on the other.

You'll have a hack to show

A first GRPO run often finds a way to score without doing the task. The GeoGuessr environment write-up found its model learning to stop exploring. Spotting yours, and fixing the reward, is the part people learn from.

What makes the comparison fair

  • Every model gets the same safes, the same move budget and the same text: the dials, the target, the moves left, and a turn tool.
  • Wiring shapes in the test set never appear in training (deduplicated up to relabelling the dials).
  • Three seeds, and a 95% interval on every rate.
  • Cost comes from public token prices for API models and the rental price of your GPU for yours.

What could go wrong, and the fallback

  • Frontier models already crack most safes. If your Stage 2 number is 85% or more, switch the headline to the sticky-pin safes and give every model a 2× budget. On those, even the probing script reaches only 60% on 5 dials.
  • GRPO groups all score the same, so nothing is learned. Turn the difficulty dial (fewer dials, a bigger budget) until about half the groups have mixed rewards. Stage 3 sets this up.
  • The gain sits inside the error bar. More held-out safes and three seeds. If it still overlaps, the honest post says so, and the curves are still worth showing.
