LLM basics study plan · Day 3

Scores → text

Day 3 of the LLM basics path: the last vector becomes a score for every token in the vocabulary, a decoder picks one, and the model runs again. Ends with you running the whole route yourself and a checkpoint.

By the end of all of this, you'll be able to
  • Explain how the last hidden vector becomes 151,936 logits, and how softmax turns them into probabilities.
  • Compare greedy, temperature, top-k, top-p and beam search on one real step, and pick one for a given job.
  • Explain degeneration: why always-greedy text loops and why pure sampling wanders.
  • Explain autoregressive generation, the probability of a whole answer, and why the KV cache exists.
  • Run the route on your own machine and change one stop.
The same routine every phase
  1. Prime (5 min): read the phase's intro and each card's green Prime box. Nothing else yet.
  2. Read in order. Cards first, then the paper with its listed sections only.
  3. Say it back at the end of each day, closed book, before checking.
