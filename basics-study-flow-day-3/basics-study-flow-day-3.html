LLM basics study path · Day 3 · one page

Scores → text

Day 3 of the LLM basics path: the last vector becomes a score for every token in the vocabulary, a decoder picks one, and the model runs again. Ends with you running the whole route yourself and a checkpoint. Everything is inline: just scroll. Ticks are shared with the checklist version.

What today uses, and why

The block's output is still just a vector. Today it becomes words: scores, probabilities, a choice, and the loop that turns one token into an answer.

Two cards written for this pathDecoding and the loop.
All five decoders measured on the same step, and the loop's cost measured on a Mac.
Two papersHoltzman et al. (top-p) and GPT-3.
Why decoding matters, and the idea that everything is text the model continues.
The lab and checkpointRun it, change it, then check yourself.
Running the route yourself is the test of whether you understand it.
By the end of today, you'll be able to
  • Explain how the last hidden vector becomes 151,936 logits, and how softmax turns them into probabilities.
  • Compare greedy, temperature, top-k, top-p and beam search on one real step, and pick one for a given job.
  • Explain degeneration: why always-greedy text loops and why pure sampling wanders.
  • Explain autoregressive generation, the probability of a whole answer, and why the KV cache exists.
  • Run the route on your own machine and change one stop.
How to use this page
  1. Scroll. The bar pinned at the top always says what the current step is and how to read it: Read Skim Revisit Checkpoint Build Task.
  2. Each phase opens with a Before you read box: the questions to read with, and a link to try the phase's checkpoint cold.
  3. The first step of each phase is a card from this hub, shown inline with its Prime and Study boxes. Most readings after it are outside pages that open in a new tab. A few load inline.
  4. Tick Done as you finish a step. Take a break after each checkpoint.
  5. Checkpoint answers stay closed until you open them. Each one says which source to reopen if you missed it.
