LLM basics study plan · Day 2

Inside the block

Day 2 of the LLM basics path: the transformer block, which repeats 24 times in our model. First how it knows word order, then attention (tokens reading each other), then the feed-forward part (each token on its own), where most of the weights live. Now Attention Is All You Need reads well.

By the end of all of this, you'll be able to
  • Show why attention on its own can't tell word order, and compare sinusoids, learned positions, RoPE and ALiBi.
  • Compute one attention head by hand, and say what Q, K and V are, beyond the slogan.
  • Explain why grouped-query attention shrinks memory and what FlashAttention speeds up, without the maths.
  • Say what the feed-forward network does to each token, why it holds most of the weights, and how a mixture of experts changes that.
The same routine every phase
  1. Prime (5 min): read the phase's intro and each card's green Prime box. Nothing else yet.
  2. Read in order. Cards first, then the paper with its listed sections only.
  3. Say it back at the end of each day, closed book, before checking.
