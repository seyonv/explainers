LLM basics study path · Day 1 · one page

Text → vectors

Day 1 of the LLM basics path: what happens to your text before the model does any thinking. It's cut into tokens, each token becomes an integer, and each integer becomes a vector. You follow one question, “What is the capital of India?”, through a real model the whole way. Everything is inline: just scroll. Ticks are shared with the checklist version.

What today uses, and why

Most courses start with the model's architecture. This path starts where your text starts, and follows it. Today covers everything that happens before the first layer.

The route cardWritten for this path.
One question traced through a real model at every stop. Each later card fills in one of its rows.
Zero to Hero tokenizer cardsFrom Karpathy's build-along series.
The clearest explanation of BPE in the hub, with numbers you can check.
Two papersBPE (2015) and word2vec (2013).
Short, readable, and the original source of both ideas. Read only the parts listed.
By the end of today, you'll be able to
  • Trace the whole route from text to answer once, and say the shape of the data at each stop.
  • Explain why models read tokens rather than letters or words, and how byte-pair encoding learns its pieces.
  • Say what a token ID is, and why it has to become a vector before the model can use it.
  • Explain why related tokens end up close together in embedding space, and what that does and doesn't tell you.
