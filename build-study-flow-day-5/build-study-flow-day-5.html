Build an LLM study path · Day 5 · one page

Make it useful

Day 5 of the Build an LLM path: Raschka chapters 6 and 7 and appendix E. Fine-tune your pretrained model twice, into a classifier and then into an assistant that follows instructions, score it with another LLM, and do the same more cheaply with LoRA. Everything is inline: just scroll. Ticks are shared with the checklist version.

What today uses, and why

A pretrained model only continues text. Today you fine-tune it to classify, then to follow instructions, then do it cheaply with LoRA.

Raschka chapters 6 and 7, appendix EFine-tuning.
The two kinds of fine-tuning, an LLM judge, and LoRA.
Two cards written for this pathInstruction tuning and LoRA.
The ideas behind the code, measured on a real model.
By the end of today, you'll be able to
  • Explain the difference between classification fine-tuning and instruction fine-tuning.
  • Format instruction data, batch it with padding, and mask the loss so only the answer is graded.
  • Score a fine-tuned model's answers with a local judge model, and name two ways that can mislead.
  • Explain LoRA: what is frozen, what is trained, and why it saves memory.
How to use this page
  1. Scroll. The bar pinned at the top always says what the current step is and how to read it: Read Skim Revisit Checkpoint Build Task.
  2. Each phase opens with a Before you read box: the questions to read with, and a link to try the phase's checkpoint cold.
  3. The first step of each phase is a card from this hub, shown inline with its Prime and Study boxes. Most readings after it are outside pages that open in a new tab. A few load inline.
  4. Tick Done as you finish a step. Take a break after each checkpoint.
  5. Checkpoint answers stay closed until you open them. Each one says which source to reopen if you missed it.
