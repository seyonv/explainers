Study plan · Day 8

Scale out: one model on many GPUs

Day 8 of the inference study plan: what to do when one GPU isn't enough. It starts with Part 4 of the request-scheduler course, then covers the Serving across GPUs course (splitting a model, splitting prefill from decode, mixture of experts) and Serving station 6.

By the end of all of this, you'll be able to
  • Rank the ways to raise one GPU's throughput before adding GPUs.
  • Explain how GPUs exchange data during inference and what the connection between them limits.
  • Split a dense model across GPUs with tensor and pipeline parallelism, and say what each costs when serving.
  • Decide when to put prefill and decode on different GPUs, and what moving the KV cache between them costs.
  • Explain mixture-of-experts routing and expert parallelism, and why uneven load across experts hurts.
  • Size a deployment: how many GPUs, and which layout, for a model, its traffic and a latency target.
The same routine every phase
  1. Prime (5–10 min): skim headings and each card's Prime box, nothing else.
  2. Pretest: try the phase's checkpoint cold. Getting it wrong tells your brain what to look for.
  3. Read in order, and do each card's Retrieve questions with the card closed.
  4. Take the checkpoint for real. Open “Go deeper” only for cards whose questions you missed.
